Names are written by people, and people add accents, capitals and spaces. The address of a series is made from its name with those removed, so two spellings of one name end up at one address.

## Same address, same series

:::callout{type="info" title="What this part checks"}
This file spells the series with accents. When the series of the first three parts exists, the import joins it instead of making a second one.
:::
